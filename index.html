01 / About

Calm systems, clear boundaries

I’m a backend engineer and team lead with 5+ years building and modernizing high-load systems, currently in fintech.

I’ve twice rebuilt legacy monoliths into event-driven microservices, and now lead the team integrating Finstar Financial Group’s platform solution into an international lending product. I stay hands-on: I write production services myself, run the infrastructure, and bring AI agents into how the team works.

The stack is C#/.NET and Go, with Kafka, Temporal, PostgreSQL, MongoDB and Redis on Kubernetes, and observability through OpenTelemetry. I use Domain-Driven Design, Clean Architecture and event-driven design so the boundaries stay clear as the system grows.

I take part in hiring and assessments, onboard and mentor new engineers, and I’m often the person colleagues ask for a second opinion on a design. I’m open to international roles that combine development with technical leadership.
