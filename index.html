01 / About

Calm systems, clear boundaries

I’m a Senior Software Engineer and Team Lead with 4+ years of building and scaling high-performance backend systems, mostly in fintech and product-driven teams.

I modernise legacy platforms, design microservices and event-driven architectures, and apply Domain-Driven Design and Clean Architecture so systems stay reliable, maintainable and easy to evolve.

My core is .NET (C#) with PostgreSQL, SQL Server, MongoDB and Redis, running on Docker, Kubernetes and CI/CD — with real observability through OpenTelemetry, Prometheus and Jaeger. I’m also building with Go for concurrent, cloud-native services.

Beyond code, I enjoy technical leadership: mentoring developers and driving architecture decisions that make systems faster and teams more productive.
